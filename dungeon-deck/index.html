📖 How it works

1. Both you and your rival begin with three drawn cards.
2. Click any totem card, then a glowing slot to summon it. You may replace your own totem when the board is full.
3. Select spells and click the highlighted enemy or ally; some spells trigger instantly.
4. Each used or discarded card starts its own 5-second cooldown before a replacement is drawn.
5. Both teams fight automatically and simultaneously. Shields absorb damage, poison ticks, guards protect the summoner, and empty lanes deal face damage.
6. Select Easy, Normal, or Hard from the top bar. Changing difficulty starts a fresh run.
7. Open Edit Deck to build your personal loadout. After each victory, take one enemy spell/protection/totem by swapping out a card. Play Fusion Combo, then click two friendly totems to merge them into one super totem.
8. Defeat nine rivals to earn nine coins, then insert all nine into the mustached vending machine to unlock the tenth and final boss. Space pauses; 1, 2, 3 selects a hand slot; Esc cancels selection.
